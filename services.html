Our Services

Cape Town Football Academy offers different training options to help players improve their football skills.

Individual training allows players to work on their own skills with a coach. This is perfect for players who want extra attention on specific techniques.

Players can take part in team training and matchdays. This helps players improve their teamwork, communication and match skills in a real game environment.

Holiday Clinics give young players the opportunity to train and improve their football skills during school holidays in a fun and intensive setting.
